# Research Report: Comparison of Sparse and Dense Retrieval Methods

## Executive Summary
This report synthesizes findings on the effectiveness and use cases of sparse retrieval methods, such as BM25, compared to dense retrieval methods based on neural networks. Sparse retrieval methods excel in scenarios requiring precise keyword matching and are particularly effective for queries involving rare terms and exact matches. BM25, a prominent sparse retrieval algorithm, is widely used in systems like Elasticsearch and Solr. In contrast, dense retrieval methods leverage semantic understanding, enabling better performance on paraphrase and synonym-heavy queries. However, they often require more computational resources and may struggle with rare terms. Hybrid approaches that combine both methods can optimize performance across various query types.
