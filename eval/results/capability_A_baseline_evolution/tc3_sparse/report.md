# Research Report: Comparing Sparse Retrieval (BM25) and Dense Retrieval Methods

## Executive Summary
This report synthesizes findings on the differences between sparse retrieval methods, specifically BM25, and dense retrieval methods, particularly those based on neural networks. BM25 is a widely used probabilistic retrieval model that excels in exact-match queries and scenarios involving rare terms. It operates effectively within traditional search systems, leveraging an inverted index for efficient keyword matching. In contrast, dense retrieval methods utilize deep neural networks to project queries and documents into a continuous vector space, enabling semantic matching. While dense methods can improve retrieval accuracy and scalability, they may struggle with exact matches and rare terms. The choice between these methods often depends on the specific context and type of queries, with hybrid approaches increasingly being adopted to combine the strengths of both.
