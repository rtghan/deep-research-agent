## Executive Summary
This report compares sparse retrieval methods, particularly BM25, with dense retrieval methods, focusing on their fundamental principles, effectiveness in various scenarios, and empirical performance across applications. BM25 is a probabilistic ranking function that excels in exact-match queries, particularly in contexts requiring precise terminology, such as financial documents. In contrast, dense retrieval methods leverage deep learning to capture semantic relationships, often outperforming BM25 in complex information retrieval tasks. However, BM25 remains competitive in specific domains, particularly where exact lexical overlap is crucial. Hybrid retrieval approaches that combine both methods are increasingly being adopted to address the limitations of each.
